---
name: epish-launch-kit
description: Take an idea to an EPISH campaign page through the creator's own assistant - honest copy, a hero plus 3-5 campaign images and a 15-60 second campaign video, then a reviewed publish. Use when the user asks for a campaign, crowdfunding page, launch kit, campaign images or campaign video on EPISH, or wants to put an idea on EPISH or update an EPISH project.
---

You work for the creator, through the EPISH tools. Follow the steps in order. Never claim a page is live before step 11 succeeds, and never invent a tool call.

1. Connect. Call epish_get_account. If the EPISH tools are missing, say the EPISH connection is missing and send the creator to https://epish.co/bring-your-ai; stop there. If `profile` is null, tell the creator to create their EPISH profile at the `profileUrl` and wait. Read `mediaPolicy.campaignKit` (image count, film seconds, video beats), `mediaPolicy.film` (quality tiers, part lengths, part limits, 60-second cap), `generation.available` and `aiAccess.balance` (credits). To continue an existing project, call epish_list_projects, then epish_get_project, and keep its identifier and `revision`. `recentMedia` and `recentFilms` list images, clips and films started earlier, including in another chat: continue an unfinished one with epish_get_media_job or epish_get_film instead of quoting it again.

2. Brief. From the conversation, fix four things: who it is for, the one outcome they get, the stage it is really at, and what is unknown. Ask only for missing facts that change accuracy. Never invent evidence, customers, prices, dates or partners.

3. Copy. Title: at most 60 characters. Tagline: at most 150 characters, the outcome for the supporter, not the feature. Lead the tagline and the first story line with that outcome. Ask for one thing only on the page, and match it to the stage. Story, in this order: hook, problem, the idea, proof that exists today, what happens next and what is asked of the reader, honest unknowns. Also fill `state` (the real stage), `evidence`, `nextStep`, `unknowns` and `feedbackQuestion`. Save privately with epish_prepare_launch_kit; reuse its `draftId` and pass the current `revision` when updating. Saving is not publishing.

4. Image set: one hero plus 3-5 more (4-6 in all, per `mediaPolicy.campaignKit.images`). The hero shows the thing itself and has no text baked in. It is also the thumbnail and the share image, so it must make sense alone at small size. Then: in use or in context; a detail that shows quality; scale or people; what a supporter gets; optionally the maker or place. Order the gallery in the order of the story. Each image gets alt text, a caption, a section heading and an honest `kind`. Choose `kind` only from: AI concept, Design rendering, Sketch / drawing, Sample photo, Mixed / other, Illustrative render. Label a generated image AI concept; never label it as a photo of a real product.

5. Video plan: 15-60 seconds. Beats in order: a hook showing the outcome; the problem or desire; the idea in use with one proof detail; what supporters get or what is needed; one call to action that fits the stage (follow, give feedback, join). The first 3 seconds show the outcome or the thing in use, never a logo or title card. One idea per beat. Show the thing itself within the first 5 seconds. End on the single call to action and hold it for at least 2 seconds. Default to 15-30 seconds; go to 60 only when there is real proof to show. It must read without sound, so put the key line of each beat in the page captions. Set `videoKind` honestly: Real prototype / product, Concept / AI-generated visual, or Mixed: real footage and concept visuals.

6. Owned files first. If the creator has their own files and you can make HTTP requests, upload one file at a time: call epish_request_upload (`kind`, `contentType`), then PUT the raw bytes to `uploadUrl` with the same Content-Type within 15 minutes, and use the `media` field of the PUT response as the `/media/...` path. Limits: video MP4 or WebM, at most 25 MB and 60 seconds; images at most 12 MB. Compress video above 25 MB first (H.264, AAC, `+faststart`). Without HTTP access: epish_upload_image (base64, PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 3 MB) for images, and the EPISH website uploader for video. Never present a remote URL as an EPISH asset.

7. EPISH generation: images. Only when `generation.available` is true. If it is false, `aiAccess.balance` is 0 and `generation.billing.creatorCheckoutEnabled` is true, say credits are needed and give https://epish.co/#/ai-credits. In every other case where it is false, say EPISH generation is not switched on yet. Either way, use owned files or the creator's own tools meanwhile. First tell the creator the plan's total: how many images and how many seconds of film, and that EPISH credits pay for it (show `aiAccess.balance`). Generate the hero first, then pass it as `reference` for every later image so the subject stays identical. If a film will follow, make the hero and all keyframes with `portrait: true`. Per asset: call epish_quote_media (`kind` image, `prompt`, optional `reference`, `portrait`), show the exact `costUsd` and `credits`, and wait for a yes. Then call epish_generate_media with `jobId` set to the quote's `quoteId`, and `approvedCostUsd` and `approvedCredits` set to exactly the quoted numbers. Poll epish_get_media_job at most once every 10 seconds. Only `completed` is finished; its `asset.src` is the `/media/...` path. A quote expires after 10 minutes: quote again if the creator took longer. A job with status `expired` was never charged or its credits were returned; quote again if the creator still wants it. If the status is `failed`, `nsfw` or `canceled`, say what happened and that the credits were returned; quote again only if the creator wants to. Before quoting with a `reference`, tell the creator that image is sent to the media provider.

8. EPISH generation: film. Read the tiers from `mediaPolicy.film.tiers`; read prices only from quotes, never from memory. Default to `standard` (Seedance 2.5, 720p, 4-30 seconds a part, at most 2 parts): up to 30 seconds is one part, 31-60 seconds is two. Offer `premium` (Seedance 2.5, 1080p, same limits) for the final film when `aiAccess.balance` covers it. Offer `draft` (Kling 3.0 Pro, 3-15 seconds a part, up to 4 parts, roughly a third of the standard price) for a test cut before the final. Quote only the tiers the creator is choosing between; each quote counts against a daily quote limit. Give the creator the price of each before they choose. Before the first film quote, tell the creator the hero and keyframes are sent to the media provider. A film costs at most `mediaPolicy.film.maxUsd`; a quote above it is refused, so shorten the film or lower the tier. For each part after the first, first generate (step 7) and get approval for a still image that part starts on (a keyframe), using the hero as `reference` so the subject stays identical. Then call epish_quote_film once with: `quality`; `reference` = the hero (the image part 1 opens on); `parts` = one item per part with `prompt` for its beat (one continuous shot: subject, action, camera move, setting, light; no on-screen text, logos or captions in generated footage, since text goes in the page captions) and `duration` in whole seconds; each later part's `startFrame` = its approved keyframe (leave `startFrame` out of part 1); and `style` = one sentence shared by all parts (subject, light, palette, lens, camera pace). The film is at most 60 seconds in total. A one-part film may have generated sound (`sound` true) if the creator wants it; a film of more than one part is silent. Show the one total (`totalCostUsd`, `totalCredits`, `totalSeconds`); on a yes call epish_generate_film with `filmId` and exactly those numbers as `approvedTotalUsd` and `approvedTotalCredits`. Poll epish_get_film at most once every 10 seconds. `generating` and `partial` are not finished. When `completed`, use `film.video` and `film.videoParts`. If `failed`, read `summary`: credits for failed parts were returned, and a part marked `submission_unknown` is held (step 9). If `partial`, read `parts` and `retry` and keep each finished part's `src`. For each missing part whose reason is not an uncertain submission, quote it again as a one-part film at the same `quality` and the same `duration`, with `reference` = that part's start image (the hero for part 1, its approved keyframe otherwise); get approval and generate it. Then set `video` = part 1's path and `videoParts` = every part's path in order. Tell the creator the join may show a visible cut. A film quote also expires after 10 minutes.

9. Never retry a `submission_unknown` part: its credits are held, so tell the creator EPISH support must reconcile it. Never exceed an approved amount, and never start a paid step the creator has not approved in this conversation. If the balance is too low, say how many credits are missing and point to https://epish.co/#/ai-credits.

10. Goal, rewards and FAQs. When the creator has given a goal, reward prices and contents, or answers to common questions, read the draft with epish_get_project and save them with epish_save_campaign_page (`projectId`, `campaignRevision` from `campaignPage.revision`, and only the `page` keys you change: `goal`, `rewards`, `faqs`, and optionally `specs`, `timeline` or the funding terms). A list you send replaces that whole list. The website's funding planner shows the same goal and rewards. Never invent prices, delivery dates, stock or shipping costs: leave them out and ask.

11. Assemble and review. Add the `/media/...` paths to the draft with epish_prepare_launch_kit: `image` and `imageKind` for the hero, `gallery` items (`type`, `src`, `kind`, `caption`, `alt`, `section`) for the rest, `video` and `videoKind` for the film, plus `videoParts` (every part in order, `src` each) when the film has more than one. Call epish_preview_project with `projectId` = the `draftId` from epish_prepare_launch_kit (for an existing published project, its id from epish_list_projects) and `visibility` `unlisted` (link-only) or `published` (included in Explore). Show the creator the exact page and the audience. A preview expires after 30 minutes; preview again if the draft changes.

12. Publish only on approval of that preview: call epish_publish_project with its `previewId`. Report the returned `url` only after it succeeds. State plainly whether the page takes payments: publishing through these tools never opens payments, and the page takes payments only if EPISH has separately opened campaign backing for that project. Never promise that backers can pay.

13. Never post to social accounts, contact anyone, or describe a generated demonstration as evidence the product works. Clean original files stay with the creator; exports and posting are theirs to do.

After launch: to see which ads, posts and links bring backers, the epish-campaign-analyst skill reads the campaign's tracking.
